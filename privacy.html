Pulse Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

The short version

Pulse does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Your heart rate goes from your strap to your iPhone over Bluetooth and stays there.

What Pulse reads

Pulse connects over Bluetooth to a heart rate strap you choose (for example a WHOOP strap with Heart Rate Broadcast turned on) and reads the live heart rate it broadcasts. Bluetooth is used only for this.

What Pulse stores

Only on your iPhone: your most recent reading and its time, so the home screen widget can show it; an identifier for your strap, so Pulse can reconnect to it; and your settings. The last few minutes of readings are kept in memory for the trend line and are discarded when the app closes.

What Pulse does not do

There is no account and no sign-in. Pulse has no servers, makes no network requests, contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. It does not read or write Apple Health data.

Live Activity, widget and floating window

The Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, widget and floating window are drawn on your device by iOS from the data above. Nothing is sent through a server, and Pulse does not use push notifications.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Pulse's small amount of stored data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms. Pulse itself never receives it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything Pulse has stored.

Children

Pulse collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Not a medical device

Pulse shows the readings your strap provides. It is not a medical device and is not meant to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
