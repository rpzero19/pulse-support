Pulse

Your live heart rate and a stress estimate: on iPhone (Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, widget) and on Windows (a small widget you can place anywhere, and the number next to the clock).

Common questions

Which straps work? Any strap that broadcasts heart rate over standard Bluetooth. With WHOOP, open the WHOOP app, go to your device settings and turn on Heart Rate Broadcast.

Pulse says "Looking for your strap". Make sure the broadcast is on and the strap is close to your iPhone. If another app or device is already connected to the strap's broadcast, close it, then tap "Find strap again" in Pulse.

The Lock Screen number disappeared. iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours. Open Pulse and it starts a new one.

How do I get the floating window? (iPhone version 1.0) Tap Open next to Floating window, or just swipe home while Pulse is open. Pinch the window to make it smaller, or slide it to the screen edge to tuck it away. Version 2 replaces it with the stress estimate on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Pulse. The widget shows your most recent reading.

How is stress worked out? From how far your heart rate over the last 2 minutes is above your resting heart rate, on a 0 to 3 scale (Low, Medium, High). It is an estimate, not WHOOP's own score. To bring it closer to what the WHOOP app shows, sit still for 2 minutes, then use Calibrate and enter WHOOP's number. Do this a few times at different moments.

Windows: where is Pulse? Drag the widget anywhere; right-click it for size, background, always on top, stress, calibration and Start with Windows. The number next to the clock is the tray icon. If you don't see it, open Settings, Personalization, Taskbar, Other system tray icons, and turn on Pulse.

Windows: Pulse can't find my strap. Make sure Bluetooth is on and Heart Rate Broadcast is on in the WHOOP app. Don't pair the strap in Windows Bluetooth settings; Pulse connects by itself. Right-click the widget and choose "Find strap again".

Is it accurate? Pulse shows exactly what your strap sends. It is not a medical device.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone or PC. Pulse has no account and no servers. See the privacy policy.
